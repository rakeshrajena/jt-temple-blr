# Temple office guide (daily use)

Sign in at **https://sjtbengaluru.org/booking/admin**.

## Every day
1. **Dashboard**: look at the five tiles — *Payments to verify*, *Awaiting approval*,
   *Services today*, *New bookings today*, *Failed WhatsApp messages*. Click a tile to open the list.
2. **Verify payments** (most important):
   - Open the booking › *Verify submitted payment*.
   - Open the temple's bank statement / UPI app and find a credit with the **same UTR** and
     **same amount**.
   - Only if you can see it: confirm the reference and amount and press *Confirm payment verified*.
     The devotee gets a WhatsApp message; the booking becomes **Confirmed** (or waits for approval).
   - If you cannot find it after a reasonable time: *Payment not found / not verified*, with a short
     reason. The devotee is asked to check and resubmit.
   - A devotee who paid at the counter: *Record payment received* with the receipt/UTR number.
3. **Approve ceremonies** (Shraddha, Upanayana, marriage, other services): after speaking with the
   priests, *Approve* (optionally with a message such as the arrival time) or *Do not approve* with
   a reason.
4. **Other Temple Services**: read the request, then *Set additional charges* with the amount. The
   devotee is told the amount and pays using their booking link.
5. **Notifications › Failed**: phone devotees whose WhatsApp message failed, then *Mark handled*.

## Booking statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Request received (not yet confirmed) | Waiting for payment and/or approval |
| Confirmed | Payment verified (or not needed) **and** approved where required |
| Not approved / Cancelled | Closed; place released for others |
| Completed | Service done (*Mark service completed* after the date) |

| Payment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Payment pending | Nothing received yet |
| Awaiting verification | Devotee entered a UTR — **not yet checked** |
| Paid (verified) | Staff (or the gateway) confirmed the money arrived |
| Payment not verified | Staff could not find it; devotee may resubmit |
| Refunded | Money returned (record the refund reference) |

## Changes
- **Reschedule**: new date and time; capacity is checked (tick *Override* only when the priests agree).
- **Cancel**: give a reason (sent to the devotee). If it was paid, return the money, then *Record refund*.
- **Add internal note**: staff-only notes, kept in the booking history.
- Every action is recorded in the booking's *History* and in the *Audit log* with your name and time.

## Reports
*Reports* shows daily, weekly or monthly bookings, booked value, verified revenue, refunds and food
coupon counts, by service and by coupon. Download CSV files (open in Excel) for accounts:
summary, line items, payments, bookings.

## Helping a devotee
- Lost booking link: they can use *Find my booking* with booking number + mobile, or copy the link
  from the booking page (*Copy devotee link*) and send it to them.
- Phone bookings: the office can make the booking on the public site on the devotee's behalf.
